Questions.

Is Ufolio really free?

Yes. Ufolio is free and open source, with no ads, no account and no paid tier.

Why does macOS warn me the first time I open it?

Ufolio isn’t signed with a paid Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS can’t check who made it. Click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security once and it opens normally from then on. Updates are signed with the project’s own key, and Ufolio checks that signature before installing anything.

How is it so fast?

Ufolio runs on WebKit, Apple’s own engine, with the speed settings Safari uses switched on. The Force Field stops ads before they download, so pages have less to fetch, and Instant pages starts loading the page you’re about to open, so it appears the moment you click.

A site doesn’t work properly. What now?

Click the Force Field button in the toolbar and lower the shield for that site. It stays lowered just for that site, and you can raise it again any time.

Can I bring my bookmarks and passwords?

Yes. The setup guide imports bookmarks and favourite sites from Safari, Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Vivaldi and Firefox, and Settings imports passwords from a CSV export.

What’s the connection with Hushlist?

Hushlist is a free blocklist of ad, tracker, phishing and malware domains. Ufolio can connect it to the Force Field in a couple of clicks, and it works on your other devices too.

How do I try the beta?

In Ufolio, open Settings › Software Update and switch on Get beta versions. Betas get new features first; fixes reach everyone in regular updates.

Wasn’t it called Hush Browser?

It was. Your settings, bookmarks, history and passwords came along for the ride when it was renamed.
